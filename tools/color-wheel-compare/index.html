Color wheel comparison

Pick a color and see where it sits on seven color wheels — and how its complement and other harmonies change from wheel to wheel.

Wheels
Click a wheel to pick the color at that point. Dots show the harmony on each wheel; the ring marks your color.
How each wheel spaces the hues
Each bar runs once around its wheel (0° → 360°). Ticks mark where pure red, yellow, green, cyan, blue and magenta land — RYB, for example, gives yellow-to-red far more room than RGB does.
Click to copy HEX
HSV, CMY, OKLCH and CIELAB follow their definitions exactly. RYB uses a common artist's mapping; the opponent (NCS-style) and PCCS wheels are screen approximations of systems defined by physical color samples. Harmonies keep your color's saturation/lightness and only turn the hue.
