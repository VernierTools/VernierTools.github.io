Use "…" or `…` around English words that would otherwise be read as romaji (name, kite …).
Good to know
  • Kanji can't be converted, because reading them needs a dictionary. They are left as they are and highlighted.
  • A word written in Latin letters is converted only if it can be read as romaji. English words like "Tower" stay as they are, but ones like "name" or "kite" are read as romaji — put them in quotes.
  • Romaji can't tell the particles は, へ, を from わ, え, お, so "wa", "e", "o" become わ, え, お ("wo" becomes を).
  • Kana to romaji treats おう and おお as one long ō, even across a word boundary (思う becomes omō).
  • Kana has no spaces between words, so the romaji comes out joined (わたしは → watashiha).
