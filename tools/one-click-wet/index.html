Blender add-on

One Click Wet

Make the materials you already have look wet in one click, using each material's own color, bumps and height. Remove Wet puts them back.

Features
  • Wet with the material's own lookColors deepen from the material's own Base Color instead of a fixed tint, and a thin water film adds reflections that follow the bumps. Works with Principled BSDF setups, including ones inside node groups.
  • Seven material profilesWood, Soil, Concrete, Asphalt, Stone, Brick and Fabric each soak, darken and reflect differently. Every setting can be fine-tuned afterwards in the sidebar.
  • Puddles and drying from heightPuddles collect in the lowest parts of the material's height map, and raised parts dry first. Lowering Wetness leaves damp islands with darker rims.
  • Soaked, Rain or GroundSoaked wets everything. Rain keeps faces under eaves dry and adds drip streaks to walls. Ground wets only up to 0.4 m above the Ground Height.
  • Animate dryingScene Wetness applies to every wet material and can be keyframed. Object Wetness changes one object even when materials are shared.
Requirements & notes

Requirements
Requires Blender 4.5 or later. Pure Python, so it should run on Windows, macOS and Linux; tested on macOS with Blender 5.2 (Cycles).

Good to know
Materials that use transparency or glass are skipped and listed in the report. Without a height or bump source, puddles and the drying pattern are left out. One undo step reverts Make Wet or Remove Wet.

Install
  1. Download the .py file on the right.
  2. In Blender, open Edit ▸ Preferences ▸ Add-ons.
  3. Open the ▾ menu at the top-right, choose "Install from Disk…", then pick the .py file.
  4. Turn the add-on on with its checkbox, select objects, then open the 3D Viewport sidebar (N) and find the "Wet" tab.
