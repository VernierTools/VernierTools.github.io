Good to know
  • JAN, EAN and UPC numbers start with a company prefix issued by GS1 (GS1 Japan for JAN). Products sold in stores need a registered number; a made-up number only works for in-house use.
  • The printed size is set by the module width (X). Shrinking a symbol below the recommended size (JAN: 0.264 mm, 80%) or cutting into the quiet zone makes it hard to scan.
  • PNG is drawn on whole pixels so the edges stay sharp. For printing, SVG keeps the exact size; check it is placed at 100% in your layout software.
  • Dark bars on a light background read best. Red bars, light bars on a dark background and low contrast often cannot be read.
  • QR Code and Data Matrix store text as UTF-8. Very old readers that expect Shift_JIS may show garbled Japanese.
  • Always test the printed symbol with a real scanner or phone before mass printing.
