How it works and its limits
  • Each channel becomes a speaker. The direct sound and the reflections off the walls, floor and ceiling (up to three bounces) reach each ear from their own direction through the browser's head-related transfer function (HRTF); the later reverb is added from the room's reverberation time.
  • The HRTF is a generic one built into the browser. Left and right are clear, distance comes through well, but how far in front the speakers seem varies from person to person.
  • Rooms are empty boxes with the same absorption on every surface. Furniture, open doors and odd shapes are not modelled.
  • Speaker types are typical frequency responses and how much treble they lose off to the side, not measurements of specific models.
  • The exported WAV is binaural: use it as the sound heard by a listener (such as the camera) in your 3D scene, on headphones.
