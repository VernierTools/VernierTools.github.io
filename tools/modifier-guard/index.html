Blender add-on

Modifier Guard

Stops heavy modifiers before they freeze the viewport.

Features
  • Stops it before it runsBlender can't cancel a modifier once it starts. Modifier Guard estimates each modifier's load just before the viewport updates, and turns off the viewport display of one that would go over the limit, so the freeze never happens.
  • Only the culpritJust the modifier that goes over the limit is stopped. Slow types like Bevel, Remesh and Exact Boolean are weighted by measured cost.
  • Calibrate for your computerOne click measures this machine and scene, then sets the limit so a modifier at the limit takes no longer than the wait you choose (2 seconds by default).
  • Easy to undoA banner tells you what was stopped. Revert to the last safe settings or Allow it for the session from the sidebar, and stopped modifiers come back on by themselves once you lower them.
  • Covers the tricky casesWorks during playback, with drivers and NLA strips, and checks heavy files right after they open. Render settings are never touched.
Requirements & notes

Requirements
Requires Blender 5.2 or later. Written for Windows, macOS and Linux, but so far tested only on macOS (Apple Silicon).

Good to know
The load is an estimate, made on the safe side, so a modifier may occasionally be stopped a little early. Geometry Nodes are estimated from their last result. Linked data from another file can't be stopped; it is listed in the panel instead.

Install
  1. Download the .zip file on the right. Don't unzip it.
  2. In Blender, open Edit ▸ Preferences ▸ Get Extensions.
  3. Open the ⌄ menu at the top-right, choose "Install from Disk…", then pick the .zip file. (Dragging the .zip into the Blender window also works.)
  4. Find it in the 3D Viewport ▸ Sidebar (N) ▸ "Modifier Guard" tab. Press Calibrate once to fit the limit to your computer.
