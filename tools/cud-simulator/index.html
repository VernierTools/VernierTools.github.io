What this can and cannot tell you

Not a medical device and not a certified or approved tool. It cannot diagnose anything, and it is not a substitute for an eye examination.

Read which colours collide, not the colours themselves. The residual hues come from reports by people with vision in only one eye affected, so the output colour is an estimate; the pairs that merge are the reliable part.

Your display is not calibrated, and published models tend to simulate a milder loss than people actually report. Treat a passing result as a floor, not a guarantee, and check with real people where the stakes are high.

Colour vision varies between individuals with the same type. P/D/T each cover a wide range.
